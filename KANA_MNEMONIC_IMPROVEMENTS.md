# Kana Mnemonic Improvement Suggestions

This document contains suggestions for improving Kana mnemonic descriptions to make them more intuitive and easier to visualize without images.

## Analysis Criteria
- **Good**: Mnemonics that are intuitive and easy to visualize (e.g., "FIND THE U IN う")
- **Needs Improvement**: Mnemonics that are abstract, hard to visualize, or don't clearly relate to the character's shape

## DAKUTEN & HANDAKUTEN

### Current Pattern
All dakuten and handakuten characters currently use: "X is Y with a dakuten/handakuten mark"

### Suggestion
These are actually fine as they clearly explain the relationship. However, we could add:
- "GA is KA (か) with two dots - makes it sound harder"
- "PA is HA (は) with a circle - makes it sound like P"

---

## COMBO CHARACTERS

### Current Pattern
All combo characters use: "X is Y + small Z"

### Suggestion
These are clear and functional. Could enhance with:
- "KYA is KI (き) + small YA (や) - combine the sounds"
- Emphasize the visual combination aspect

## RECOMMENDED APPROACH

1. **Connect Sound to Image**: Use words that contain the target sound (e.g., "KITE" for "ka", "KEG" for "ke")
2. **Make It Vivid**: Create memorable, visual stories that help users picture the character
3. **Tie Shape to Sound**: The mnemonic should help users remember both the shape AND the sound
4. **Use Bold for Sound Cues**: Mark the relevant sound in the mnemonic text (e.g., **KI**TE, **KE**G)
5. **Keep It Intuitive**: The image should be easy to visualize and relate to the character's shape

---

## NOTES

- Good mnemonics connect the sound to a visual image using words that contain that sound
- Examples: "A **KI**TE with a string" (ka), "A **KE**G of beer" (ke), "**SA**-BOTAGED" (sa)
- The mnemonic should help users both see the shape and hear the sound
- Katakana characters need individual review since they can look very different from Hiragana
